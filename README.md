AEC projects are complex. The tools used to run them shouldn’t make the work harder.

**IntoAEC brings project delivery, business growth, procurement, and finances into one connected platform**—so architects, engineers, contractors, and construction teams can spend less time chasing information and more time moving projects forward.

## One platform for the full project journey

- **Plan and deliver:** manage tasks, schedules, time, project files, and daily progress.
- **Measure and estimate:** work with 2D takeoffs, Bills of Quantities (BoQs), estimates, and project budgets.
- **Win and manage work:** organize leads, build proposals, collect project requirements, and stay connected with clients.
- **Coordinate people and purchasing:** work with teams, vendors, procurement, and project materials.
- **Keep the numbers moving:** manage invoices, bills, expenses, payments, and business dashboards.

AI assistance supports practical workflows such as creating estimates and drafting proposals. The goal is simple: make the business side of building more connected and manageable.

## Built for the people who build

IntoAEC is designed for **architects, interior designers, engineers, contractors, builders, and construction businesses**—as well as owners and developers who need clearer project visibility.

## Why IntoAEC

AEC work has its own language, handoffs, and on-the-ground realities. IntoAEC is built around those workflows, helping teams bring project information and everyday operations together instead of scattering them across disconnected tools.
